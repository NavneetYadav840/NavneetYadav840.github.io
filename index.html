About

Synthetic inorganic materials scientist and analytical chemist with over four years of post-PhD research experience developing noble-metal and multimetallic nanomaterials for biomedical sensing, antimicrobial and anticancer applications, and electrochemical wastewater treatment. Former Royal Society Newton International Fellow, experienced in translating fundamental materials concepts into experimentally validated solutions, with expertise in advanced characterisation, mechanistic interpretation, grant development, laboratory management and postgraduate mentoring.
