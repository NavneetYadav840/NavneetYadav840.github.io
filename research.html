Research Areas

🧬

Biomedical nanomaterials & sensing

Noble-metal, multimetallic and magnetic nanoparticles designed for diagnosis and therapy.

  • Ag@Au nanostars and magnetic nanoparticles for an early-stage pancreatic-cancer nanosensor
  • Trimetallic Au/Pt/Cu, Au/Pt/Ag and Au/Pt/Pd nanofluids with strong antibacterial activity
  • Mushroom-mediated Au, Ag and Au–Pt nanoparticles active against colon-cancer cell lines
  • Experimental results supported by DFT insights into biological activity

💧

Electrochemical & plasma water treatment

Removing persistent “forever chemicals” and antibiotics from wastewater (Royal Society Newton Fellowship).

  • Nanostructured Ti, Sb-doped SnO2 and Pt-modified anodes for electrochemical oxidation
  • Non-thermal cold plasma and ozonation to degrade PFOA, azithromycin and erythromycin
  • HPLC-MS identification of by-products and degradation pathways
  • Linking anode composition and surface chemistry (XPS, XRD, Raman) to treatment performance

⚗️

Green & microwave-assisted synthesis

Fast, energy-efficient routes to complex multimetallic nanostructures with tunable properties.

  • Microwave-assisted chemical reduction of noble mono-, bi- and trimetallic nanoparticles
  • Granted Indian patent for green synthesis of trimetallic Au/Pt/Cu nanoparticles
  • Formation of trimetallic particles from bimetallic nano-islands
  • Structure–property relationships from XRD, XPS, TEM/HRTEM and SEM-EDS mapping

Functional & energy materials

Collaborative work on oxides, sensors and alloys for energy and electronic applications.

  • High-entropy oxide electrocatalysts for supercapatteries and water electrolysis
  • Humidity sensors from Au nanoparticles and MoS2/carbon-nanotube composites
  • Mechanical, elastic and thermophysical properties of high-entropy and Ir–Re alloys
  • Ultrasonic attenuation and thermal conduction in bimetallic nanofluids
